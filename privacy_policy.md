# Privacy Policy for pfSense Logger
Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how pfSense Logger ("we", "us", or "the extension") collects, uses, and protects your information. This extension is designed for network administration and security auditing, specifically to log and monitor pfSense web session activity.

## 1. Information We Collect
To fulfill its primary function, the extension automatically collects and transmits the following specific data points when active:

Authentication Information: Active session IDs used to authenticate with the pfSense firewall interface.

Network Information: The IP address of the target pfSense firewall (pfsense_ip2) and the client IP address initiating the connection.

Device Information: Basic browser identification data (e.g., "Chrome").

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The data collected is used strictly for the single purpose of the extension: logging, auditing, and monitoring administrative access to designated pfSense network infrastructure. We do not use this data for any other purpose, such as analytics, user profiling, or feature testing.

## 3. How Your Information is Transmitted and Stored
Data is transmitted securely from your browser directly to a privately owned, developer-controlled logging server using HTTPS encryption. Once received, the data is stored in a private database (SQLite) strictly for internal administrative review.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties. The data remains entirely within the control of the network administrator utilizing the extension and the logging server.

## 5. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure
The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension permissions will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, we affirm that:

Allowed Use: We only use the collected data to provide the core administrative logging features of the extension.

No Selling: We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties, data brokers, or any other entity.

No Advertising: We do not use or transfer your data for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.

No Creditworthiness: We do not use or transfer your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

## 6. Data Security
We implement standard security measures to protect the safety of your administrative data. All communications between the extension and the logging server are encrypted via TLS/SSL (HTTPS), ensuring that sensitive session identifiers cannot be intercepted in transit.
